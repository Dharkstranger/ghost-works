# Words

One word for each thing, on every screen and in every message.

| Word | What it is | Never |
|---|---|---|
| **Work** | one painting, print or sculpture on consignment | item, piece, asset, artwork |
| **Artist** | the person who made the work and lent it | consignor (on screen) |
| **Gallery** | the place holding and selling the work | consignee, dealer |
| **Book** | the shared list of works between one artist and one gallery | ledger, database, dashboard |
| **Handover** | a work changing hands, with a photo and both names | transfer, event, transaction |
| **Ghost** | a work nobody can currently account for | error, warning, alert |
| **Share** | the artist's part of a sale, in naira | payout, commission split |
| **Paid** | the artist's share has reached them, with a date | settled, reconciled |

Rules: plain words a busy gallery owner reads in one glance. No sentence
where one word does. Buttons say what happens ("Hand over", "Mark paid").
Never jargon. Every word on screen lives in `src/words.ts`, not in a component.
